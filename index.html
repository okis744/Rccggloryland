ABOUT US

A place where faith comes alive.

Welcome to RCCG Gloryland Parish. We are a community of people seeking God, growing together and serving others through the love of Christ.

Our desire is to create an environment where everyone can worship freely, discover God's purpose and build meaningful relationships.

Meet Our Pastor

Our parish pastor is committed to leading the church family in worship, prayer, God's Word and service.

Through faithful leadership and care for the congregation, our pastor encourages everyone to grow in faith and live out the love of Christ.

“Serving God's people with love, faith and grace.”
Pastor E.A. Adeboye General Overseer, RCCG Worldwide
RCCG WORLDWIDE

Our General Overseer

Pastor Enoch Adejare Adeboye serves as the General Overseer of The Redeemed Christian Church of God worldwide.

RCCG's official history records that Pastor Adeboye joined the church in 1973, was ordained in 1975, and later succeeded Pa Josiah Akindayomi as General Overseer.

“We want to make heaven and take as many people with us.”
